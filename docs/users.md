## User
Olivia is a greyhound racing enthusiast who follows GBGB race schedules and betting opportunities.
She wants to make informed, evidence-based betting decisions but has limited time to manually research every dog's racing history, compare performances, and assess the competitive conditions of each race.
Her primary goal is to identify the most statistically reliable betting opportunity in any given race, filtering out hype and relying on historical data, measurable performance indicators, and objective evidence.

## User Journal
Olivia receives a notification or discovers online that a new GBGB greyhound race is scheduled for the upcoming weekend.
Interested in placing a bet, she wants to assess the race without spending hours manually researching every participating greyhound.
She copies the race details URL and pastes it into HoundIQ.
HoundIQ analyses the available race data and generates an evidence-backed assessment, identifying the most statistically consistent contender as the Anchor Bet. It explains the reasoning behind its confidence score by highlighting relevant indicators, such as historical win rates from the current trap, sectional time consistency, recent class drops, and other relevant performance patterns.
Olivia reviews the statistical breakdown, understands the evidence behind the recommendation, and makes her betting decision within minutes.
